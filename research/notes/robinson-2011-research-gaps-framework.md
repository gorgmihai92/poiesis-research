# Development of a framework to identify research gaps from systematic reviews (Robinson, Saldanha & Mckoy, 2011)

## What it says (from the abstract)
- Few of the organisations reviewed used an explicit framework to find research gaps: 4 evidence-based practice
  centres (33%) and 3 other organisations (8%). Variations of PICO (population, intervention, comparison, outcomes)
  were the most common.
- The framework describes a gap with the PICOS elements, which add the setting, and names why the gap exists:
  insufficient or imprecise information, biased information, inconsistency or unknown consistency, or not the
  right information.

## What it means for the plugin
- The evidence table in `playbooks/evidence.md` has one row per study and the PICOS elements as columns, in plain
  words: who was studied, what was varied, compared with what, what was measured, in what setting.
- Research fills the table and says which combinations the search found no study for. Naming why a gap exists is
  interpretation and belongs to poiesis-insight.
- Abstract only: the columns come from the abstract's list. How the authors apply the framework was not read.
