# New evidence pyramid (Murad, Asi, Alsawas & Alahdab, 2016)

- **Source:** BMJ Evidence-Based Medicine 21(4): 125–127 · https://doi.org/10.1136/ebmed-2016-110401 (full text
  from Europe PMC, PMC4975798; no page numbers, cited by section)
- **File:** `../papers/murad-2016-new-evidence-pyramid.txt`
- **Licence:** CC BY-NC 4.0: can be kept and quoted with attribution, non-commercially.
- **Evidence:** analytical (perspective) · builds on the GRADE framework.

## What it says
- The traditional pyramid ranks evidence by study design; GRADE showed that certainty depends on many factors, so
  the lines between designs become "wavy": a study can be rated down (bias, imprecision) or up (large, consistent
  effects) whatever its design (figure 1, "Rationale for modification 1").
- Systematic reviews and meta-analyses are taken off the top: they are "a lens through which evidence is viewed",
  and their results depend on analytical choices (figure 1, "Rationale for modification 2").

## What it means for the plugin
- The quality list in rules.md should not be a strict ranking: the design gives a starting point, then the
  individual study's quality (bias, sample, precision, fit) moves it up or down; a review is only as good as the
  studies it combines.
