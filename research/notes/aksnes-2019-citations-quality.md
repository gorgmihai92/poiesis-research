## What it says (from the abstract)
- Research quality has several dimensions: solidity, originality, scientific value, societal value.
- Citations reflect aspects of scientific impact and relevance, with important limits, and give no clear evidence
  of the other dimensions.

## What it means for the plugin
- Supports "many citations mean influence, not truth": citation counts rank influence; soundness and fit are
  judged by reading.
