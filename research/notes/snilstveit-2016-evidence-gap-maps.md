## What it says (from the abstract)
- Evidence on a sector is often scattered across databases, websites and grey literature, and the lack of an
  overview is a barrier to using it and to spending research money well.
- An evidence and gap map collects the evidence in a framework of the interventions and outcomes that matter in a
  sector. It gives a visual overview of what is known and not known, and links to summaries of the studies.
- The map shows the gaps: where little or no evidence from impact evaluations and systematic reviews is available.

## What it means for the plugin
- The evidence table is saved as a file in `research/maps/`, so the overview outlasts the conversation and
  poiesis-insight can read it.
- The map shows where the search found studies and where it found none. An empty combination is reported as "none
  found by this search", with the search named, never as "does not exist".
