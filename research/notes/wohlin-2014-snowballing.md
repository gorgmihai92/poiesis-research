# Guidelines for snowballing in systematic literature studies and a replication in software engineering (Wohlin, 2014)

- **Source:** Proceedings of EASE 2014 · https://doi.org/10.1145/2601248.2601268
- **File:** none (reference only)
- **Licence:** © ACM: closed.
- **Evidence:** analytical guidelines, evaluated by replicating a published review · **abstract and a library guide's summary only**.

## What it says (from the abstract)
- Start from a set of papers, follow their references (backward) and citations (forward), include or exclude,
  repeat until nothing new appears; snowballing can be a first search strategy instead of database searches.

## What it means for the plugin
- Supports starting from a field's top works and their reference lists (the founding-works step) rather than from
  keyword searches alone. Forward snowballing (who cites a key work) is `citing.py`: later replications and
  critiques of the studies a verdict rests on, and of founding works the user keeps.
