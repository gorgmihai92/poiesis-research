# Sources behind the rules

The sources behind the rules in `skills/research/rules.md` ("Where these rules come from"). One note per source in `notes/`; open-licence files in `papers/` (closed ones stay local).

- **Citations made by language models:** [Walters & Wilder 2023](notes/walters-2023-fabricated-citations.md),
  55% / 18% fabricated (GPT-3.5 / GPT-4). CC BY.
- **Quality of evidence:** [Murad et al. 2016](notes/murad-2016-new-evidence-pyramid.md), new evidence pyramid.
  CC BY-NC.
- **Citations and quality:** [Aksnes et al. 2019](notes/aksnes-2019-citations-quality.md), citations measure
  influence. CC BY, abstract only.
- **Database coverage:** [Martín-Martín et al. 2021](notes/martin-martin-2021-database-coverage.md), Google Scholar
  88%; [Culbert et al. 2025](notes/culbert-2025-openalex-references.md), OpenAlex references comparable to WoS and
  Scopus. arXiv: local only.
- **Finding foundations:** [Small 1973](notes/small-1973-co-citation.md), co-citation;
  [Wohlin 2014](notes/wohlin-2014-snowballing.md), snowballing, backward and forward. Closed: references only.
- **Searching against a claim:** [Nickerson 1998](notes/nickerson-1998-confirmation-bias.md), confirmation bias.
  Closed.
- **Non-academic sources:** [Wineburg & McGrew 2019](notes/wineburg-2019-lateral-reading.md), lateral reading.
  Closed, abstract only.

Added for 2.4.0. Each note's **Read** line says how much of the source was read.
