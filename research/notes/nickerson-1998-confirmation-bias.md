# Confirmation Bias: A Ubiquitous Phenomenon in Many Guises (Nickerson, 1998)

- **Source:** Review of General Psychology 2(2): 175–220 · https://doi.org/10.1037/1089-2680.2.2.175

## What it says
- Confirmation bias: seeking or interpreting evidence in ways partial to existing beliefs, expectations or a
  hypothesis in hand (p. 1).
- Restricting attention to one favored hypothesis precludes reading the data as support for any alternative (p. 3).

## What it means for the plugin
- A search shaped by the claim ("<claim> meta-analysis") finds what supports it. The evidence playbook searches
  against the claim with the same effort (replications, failures, critiques, the opposite claim) and reports both.

## Quotes
- "the seeking or interpreting of evidence in ways that are partial to existing beliefs" (p. 1)
