# Co-citation in the scientific literature: A new measure of the relationship between two documents (Small, 1973)

- **Source:** Journal of the American Society for Information Science 24(4): 265–269 · https://doi.org/10.1002/asi.4630240406
- **File:** none (reference only)
- **Licence:** © Wiley: closed.
- **Evidence:** analytical with an example (particle physics) · **abstract only**.

## What it says (from the abstract)
- Co-citation: the number of later documents that cite two documents together; co-cited documents form the
  specialties of a field.

## What it means for the plugin
- The basis of `foundations.py`: works that many of a field's top works cite together are its shared foundations.
